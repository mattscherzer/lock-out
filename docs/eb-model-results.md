The committed `data/processed/lookup_v0.json` comes from the same run. The baselines and folds are the ones in `docs/backtest-results.md`; compare models on the same file.

## Model

- Gamma-Poisson Empirical Bayes: each area's count is pulled toward its Bezirksregion, and each Bezirksregion toward the city. The prior strengths are estimated from the data (method of moments). An area with few thefts is pulled more strongly.
- Tiers are relative percentile bands of areas: Low 60%, Medium 25%, High 15%. Areas with the same rate get the same tier, the higher one.
- v0 scope: time band, day group and bike value (one split at EUR 1000) are stored as citywide shares in the metadata (D-18). No area carries multiplied values, and as citywide multipliers they never change an area's tier. Each selector carries a flag from the hold-out validation (`selectors` in the metadata); the checks are in `docs/validation-report.md`.
