# Interview Guide

One place for every question the discovery steps raised. Ask about **past behavior, not future intent**; do not mention the product until the concept reaction; record exact words. Assumption IDs refer to the register in [step-0-context.md](step-0-context.md).

## Screening and recruiting

Target screen: "In the last 4 weeks, where did you leave your bike outside, for how long, and what lock did you use?" Anyone who cannot answer concretely is not the target. Aim for 8–10 interviews, including all three persona types.

| Persona | Where to find 3–4 people matching them                                                  | Screening question                                          |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lena    | Neighborhood Facebook groups, Kleinanzeigen "bike stolen" posts, flat-share chats       | "Does your bike stay outside overnight?"                    |
| Murat   | Bike parking at S-/U-Bahn stations, hospital and shift-worker networks, WhatsApp groups | "How long is your bike left at the station on a workday?"   |
| Jonas   | ADFC Berlin groups, Strava clubs, cycling Reddit, bike shops                            | "What did you pay for your bike and where do you leave it?" |

Interviews 1–5 use the Test 3 protocol in [step-4](step-4-market.md#tests-for-the-validation-phase): the same questions, then the Bike Theft Clock versus the verdict mock, in 20 minutes.

## 30-minute guide

1. **Warm-up (3 min):** how do you get around Berlin in a normal week?
2. **Last-time story (10 min):** walk me through the last time you locked your bike outside. Where, how long, which lock, what were you thinking?
3. **Theft history (5 min):** has a bike or part ever been stolen? What happened next, and what changed?
4. **Information habits (5 min):** how do you decide where and how to lock? Who or what do you ask? Have you ever used a map or app for this?
5. **Concept reaction (5 min):** show a single verdict mock screen. What would you do with it? What would you doubt?
6. **Close (2 min):** who else should I talk to?

## Question bank by topic

Pick from these inside the guide's steps. "Listen for" says which answer would weaken a hypothesis.

| Topic | Questions | Listen for | Tests |
| ----- | --------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Habit and worry | "Tell me about the last time you locked your bike and walked away. What went through your mind?" / "When did you last think about your bike being stolen?" | "Rarely" | A1 |
| Behavior change | "Has anything ever made you change how you lock?" / "What would you do differently if you could?" | "No", "nothing" | A1 |
| Lock confidence | "How do you know whether your lock is good?" / "Which lock do you own and why?" | Confidence, not doubt | A5 |
| Information habits | "What have you tried to find out whether a place is safe to lock up? How did it go?" / "Who do you talk to about this?" / "Have you used any map or app for this?" | "Nobody", no tools | A1, A2 |
| Pain and advice | "What's the most annoying thing about locking your bike?" / "Tell me about a time bike-safety advice was useful, and one when it wasn't." | Shrugs | A5 |
| Parking choice | Does the station parker have any choice of rack? Does the overnight parker check anything? | "No choice" | A3, A6 |
| Insurance | Does your insurance require a certain lock? (Jonas-type only) | "No" means drop the job | Step 1 JTBD |
| Concept reaction | Show one mock screen. "If you got 'medium risk', what would you do with it?" / "Would you rather be warned or reassured?" / what would you doubt? | "Nothing" | A2, A4 |
| Tone | Does "normal" feel reassuring or like no information? Did anything feel alarmist or unfair to a neighborhood? | "No information" | A4, A11 |

## Concept tests per idea

- **Tonight's Verdict:** show five riders a "normal" and a "higher than usual" mock (verdict sentence, lock-tier line, calm state). Ask what they would do and whether they believe it.
- **Honest Fog:** show the same area with and without the confidence label; ask how much each rider trusts the answer.
- **Settle It:** give two riders a real disagreement from their own experience (for example, two station options) and see whether the result ends the argument or is ignored.

The full test plan with pass and kill criteria is in [step-4-market.md](step-4-market.md#tests-for-the-validation-phase).
