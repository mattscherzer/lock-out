# S1 Results: OpenStreetMap Counts in a Poisson GLM

Generated by `uv run python src/models/glm.py data/raw/fahrraddiebstahl_2026-10-26.csv` from `fahrraddiebstahl_2026-10-26.csv` (not in Git), with reports up to 24 Oct 2026 and thefts counted up to the 14-day cutoff, 10 Oct 2026. The folds, history windows and scores are those of `docs/backtest-results.md`; compare models on the same file.

## Features

OpenStreetMap features inside Berlin, counted per planning area by `src/models/osm_features.py` (one Overpass request per kind, cached): bike parking: 26,687; shops: 21,638; food and drink places: 12,366; transit stops: 7,517; schools, colleges and universities: 1,338. Each area also has its size in km2 from the planning-area data. The counts table was written on 05 Nov 2026 (the date of `data/raw/osm/osm_features.csv`) from the Overpass responses cached in `data/raw/osm/cache`; a rerun with a new table shows a new date here. The counts come from the current map and are used for the earlier folds too; a model in use would only have the map of its own time. Only these aggregate counts are used. The per-area table (`data/raw/osm/osm_features.csv`) is not in Git until the ODbL question (risk R4) is answered. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

## Models

- **EB v0:** the current model (`docs/eb-model-results.md`).
- **GLM on OSM counts:** a Poisson GLM of each area's history count on log(1 + count) per kind and log(size).
- **EB toward the GLM:** EB v0's area step, with each area pulled toward its GLM prediction instead of its Bezirksregion.

### GLM coefficients

On the log scale: a coefficient of 0.5 on a kind means that doubling 1 + its count multiplies the expected thefts by about 2^0.5 = 1.41, with the other inputs fixed. Kinds that rise together, such as shops and food and drink places, share their effect, so read their coefficients together, not one by one.

## Decision checks

The roadmap keeps S1 only if it improves the hold-out deviance over EB v0 and the rankings are stable across folds. Stable is read here as: the Spearman correlation between a model's rates from the two folds is at most 0.02 below EB v0's (a working rule, not a roadmap figure).
